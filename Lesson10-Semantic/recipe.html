BBQ Chicken Quesadillas

Ingredients

  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1 lb cooked chicken
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup red onion
  • 1 jalapeño

Steps

  1. Spread BBQ sauce on one side of each tortilla.
  2. Place a portion of chicken and cheese on one half of each tortilla.
  3. Top with red onion and jalapeño.
  4. Fold the tortillas in half to create quesadillas.
  5. Then heat a pan over medium heat and wait for the outside to become golden brown and the inside to be heated through.

Why This Recipe Works

This recipe uses the tenderness and flavor of the BBQ sauce and the cheese to create a delecious meal.

Fun Fact

When the BBQ sauce is combined with the melting cheese, it pretty much carmelizes it, creating an edible glue to holds the ingredients together.
