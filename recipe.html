BBQ Chicken Quesadillas

I picked this recipe as my parents always make it on special ocasions and it is delicious and so flavorful.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1 lb cooked chicken
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup red onion
  • 1 jalapeño

Steps

    1. Spread BBQ sauce on one side of each tortilla.
    2. Place a portion of chicken and cheese on one half of each tortilla.
    3. Top with red onion and jalapeño.
    4. Fold the tortillas in half to create quesadillas.
    5. Then heat a pan over medium heat and wait for the outside to become golden brown and the inside to be heated through.

Notes

  • Equipment needed:
    • Pan or grill
    • Something to flip the quesadillas
  • Substitutions:
    • Use a different type of cheese if needed
    • Use a different type of meat if needed
